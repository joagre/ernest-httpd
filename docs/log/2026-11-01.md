## WebSocket is the server's

*2026-11-01.* The frames and the accept value are the server's own module, `WebSocket`, moved from Ernest's half: a protocol one kind of program speaks is that program's own until another asks, Ernest's log's *WebSocket Is the Server's*. It decodes messages and not frames, since the limit and the UTF-8 check belong to a message and would otherwise be split between the module and `Shell`. It carries a `Decoder` value and not bytes, since a control frame may stand between two fragments, which a decoder of one frame at a time would read again (principle 2).
