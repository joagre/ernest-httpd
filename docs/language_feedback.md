19. **G.6 does not say which characters `Json.format` escapes.** `Json.format(Json.Text("a\u{0}b\u{7f}c\td"))` writes `"a\u0000b` then U+007F bare, then `c\td"`: U+0000 to U+001F escaped, U+007F not, which a program that shows a refused string must know. Principle 1: the page is silent and the probe is the only answer. Ask: G.6 states what `format` escapes.

    Verdict of 2026-11-03, Ernest's 223: the ask, G.6 stating what `format` escapes, `"` and `\`, the short escapes for a line feed, a carriage return, a tab, a backspace and a form feed, `\u` and four uppercase hexadecimal digits for every other character below U+0020, and every other character as itself, U+007F and `/` among them; a probe on the archive from `f325872e` shows exactly that. Left standing, since that archive's G.6 and its `Ernest.Json` manual page hold no such sentence: the line goes when an installed page states it.
