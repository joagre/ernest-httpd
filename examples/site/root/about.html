About

This site shows what ernest-httpd does, on your own machine. The same server serves ernest-lang.org; here it runs with a configuration of its own, on localhost over plain HTTP.

What it answers

It serves the files under examples/site/root/ to GET and HEAD, and answers any other method 405. A directory is served as its index.html, and without its slash it answers 301 first. A file not changed since the browser last asked answers 304, and a path that leads nowhere answers 404 with the site's own 404.html. The host decides too: localhost is served, www.localhost is redirected, and any other answers 421.

Where to look

A browser hides most of this. examples/README.md gives a curl command for each answer, and the access log, build/examples/site/access.log, has a line for each response. Back to the home page, or on to the guide.
